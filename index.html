📖 How to use this generator

  1. Build your character — choose gender, skin tone, ethnicity, hair, body shape, outfit, accessories, and shoes. Hit + Add Character to lock them in. You can add multiple characters!
  2. Choose your product — select what you are creating: a t-shirt, poster, sticker, coloring page, tumbler, and more.
  3. Set the scene — pick a setting, color palette, art style, lighting, camera angle, mood, and font style. Click any option to select it. Click + Custom to type your own.
  4. Generate your prompt — hit the Generate Prompt button. Your ready-to-use AI image prompt will appear below.
  5. Copy & paste — copy the prompt and paste it directly into your favorite AI image tool:
Midjourney DALL-E 3 Adobe ChatGPT Stable Diffusion Leonardo AI

💡 Tip: Use Randomize for instant inspiration, Save to keep your favorites, and Export to download all saved prompts as a text file.
